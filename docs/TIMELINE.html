Why this order

Prove the scary things first. The deliberate move: the two places AI can’t be trusted on this product — RLS tenant isolation and cross-engine citation parsing — are front-loaded into W1 and W2. If either breaks, it surfaces in week 1–2 with five weeks of runway, not in week 6.
Critical-path dependency (can move the date). The architecture’s own #1 risk — engine access: official API vs. DataForSEO scraping — gates P2. Resolve it during P0/W1; if it’s still open when W2 starts, P2 slips and everything downstream slides with it.
Method: architecture-first, agent-consumable context, validation gates, judgment over execution. Backend: Insforge (open-source, agent-native — Postgres+pgvector, Auth, S3 storage, model gateway, MCP-driven).
